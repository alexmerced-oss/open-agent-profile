# Governance

OAP is an open, maintainer-led draft specification.

The maintainers publish their names in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Normative changes begin as public issues describing the interoperability problem, compatibility impact, schema change, conformance cases, and at least one implementation commitment. Pull requests changing normative behavior require approval from two maintainers when two are available; otherwise the sole maintainer must leave the proposal open for at least 14 days and publish a written disposition of substantive objections.

Data-model changes update the specification, schema, validator, conformance fixtures, examples, changelog, and implementation report together. Security-boundary changes additionally require a threat-model review. Normative decisions and errata are public and reversible only through the same process.
